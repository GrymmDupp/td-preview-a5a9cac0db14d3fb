The Archive shelf

A second shelf for verified dark-history places that are not for sale. For-sale books carry a red wax For sale now seal; Archive books are bound in graphite cloth with silver tooling and a pasted paper bookplate. Everything below is a style sample. No real Archive place exists yet, and nothing on this page is a place.

1. "For sale now" wax seal (real listings from the feed)

Pressed on the corner of every for-sale card and the cover of every for-sale book. "Sale pending" reads differently.

2. Bookplates and credits

3. An Archive book EXAMPLE, NOT A REAL PLACE

Graphite buckram, silver tooling, a bookplate on the cover, an "As seen on" credit that names the episode and links to where to watch it (no footage, ever). Tap the book. The name, town and show below are placeholders.

4. A private home: the town, never the pin

A private home in the Archive shows only its town. The app draws a dashed town-area circle instead of a pin, shows no street address or house photos, and the publisher rounds its coordinates to about 1 km and refuses a street address in the text. Places that are public, commercial or abandoned can carry an exact seal pin. Where flagged, the book says View from the public road only.

5. Empty state: the locked glass case

What the Archive page shows until places have been catalogued. Tap the padlock.
