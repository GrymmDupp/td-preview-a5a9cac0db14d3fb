Opening effects by category

Each listing's book plays a short flourish (2.5 seconds or less) for its category the moment it opens. Pick a category: the sample book is rebound in that category's leather and opens. The effect never covers anything you can tap, and it is skipped under reduced motion or when Effects is off.

Reveals, Nyx and the radio

Tap Play on any card. Everything here also runs in the app; all of it is still (but usable) under Reduce motion or with Effects off. Sounds need the app's Sound toggle on.
