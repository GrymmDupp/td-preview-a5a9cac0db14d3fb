TwistedPassport

Twisted History Tours · since the paper days

Remember the little passport we stamped on every tour? It’s back. Turn on the live map, press Start, and drive past a twisted place. Within about 300 feet, its stamp lands here, and its pin on the map turns into the stamp.

Never leave the road for a stamp. Many of these places are private homes with people living in them. Don’t stop, park, knock or trespass. A drive-by is all it takes.
